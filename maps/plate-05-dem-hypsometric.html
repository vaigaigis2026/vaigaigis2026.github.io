Localized elevation anomalies (e.g., −16 m) within the SRTM 30 m DEM across the Ramanathapuram coastal tract are interpreted as processing artifacts — primarily stemming from C-band synthetic aperture radar (SAR) phase decorrelation and specular reflection over saturated coastal interfaces — rather than geomorphic depressions or sub-tidal features; consequently, ground validation using high-resolution optical DEMs such as CartoDEM (30 m) or terrestrial differential GNSS (dGNSS) surveys remains necessary prior to definitive geomorphometric classification.
